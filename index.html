Lecturer · Combustion & energy safety讲师 · 燃烧与能源安全

I study how combustion and explosions occur, and how they can be suppressed. My current work focuses on hydrogen safety, lithium-ion battery vent gases, and fuel chemical kinetics, using experiments, numerical simulation, and reaction mechanism analysis.我研究燃烧与爆炸如何发生，以及如何抑制。当前工作围绕氢能安全、锂离子电池排气风险与燃料化学动力学展开，结合实验、数值模拟和反应机理分析。

01

Research研究方向

  • Hydrogen combustion & suppression氢能燃烧与抑爆

    Autoignition, explosion limits, and the failure of water mist suppression in hydrogen and ammonia-containing hydrogen flames.关注氢气及含氨氢气的自燃、爆炸极限与水雾抑制失效问题。

  • Battery vent gas safety电池排气安全

    Ignition and explosion risks of lithium-ion battery vent gases, and how gas composition affects combustion.研究锂离子电池排气的着火与爆炸风险，分析组分变化对燃烧特性的影响。

  • Fuel chemical kinetics燃料化学动力学

    Experiments and kinetic modeling of low-temperature combustion, cool flames, and surrogate aviation fuels.围绕低温燃烧、冷焰与航空燃料替代机理，结合实验和动力学建模理解反应过程。
