About简介

My research focuses on green aviation and new energy safety, with particular interest in hydrogen applications, combustion and explosion suppression, lithium-ion battery thermal runaway, and complex reaction kinetics. I combine experimental testing, numerical simulation, chemical kinetic analysis, and intelligent optimization methods to reveal the catastrophic evolution mechanisms of high-risk energy systems and to explore safer, more efficient, and more sustainable prevention and control strategies. Beyond research, I am also committed to engineering education and research training, aiming to explain complex energy safety problems in a clear, rigorous, and thoughtful way, and to strengthen the connection among academic research, engineering practice, and public understanding. 我主要从事绿色航空与新能源安全相关研究，关注氢能应用、燃烧与爆炸抑制、锂电池热失控及复杂反应动力学等问题。我的研究结合实验测试、数值模拟、化学反应机理分析与智能优化方法，致力于揭示高风险能源系统中的灾变演化规律，并探索更安全、高效、可持续的防控策略。除科研工作外，我也长期关注工程教育与科研训练，希望以清晰、严谨而富有温度的方式，将复杂的能源安全问题讲明白，推动学术研究、工程应用与公众理解之间的连接。
